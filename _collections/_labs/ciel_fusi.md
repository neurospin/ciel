The fUSi platform is dedicated to small animal neuroimaging and, besides an fUSi system, will integrate a virtual reality set-up for mice, also providing sensory stimulation and further multi-modal instrumentation.
This new facility will complete the small animals fMRI approaches mastered by CIEL lab. The fUSi is state-of-the-art neuroimaging system easy to operate on awake, task-engaged animals and with an easier learning curve than fMRI. You will find timely updates on the Univesité Paris Saclay newsletter and, soon, on the [Paris Saclay Plugin Labs webpage.](https://www.pluginlabs-universiteparissaclay.fr/en/home/)
If you need more information about this new facility or aim at developping a project with fUSi, drop an email to: [Davide Boido](mailto:davide.boido@cea.fr) or [Gwenael Pagé](mailto:gwenael.page@cea.fr)
