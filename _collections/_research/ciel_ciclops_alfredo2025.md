teasing: MRI in vivo is a versatile clinical tool for noninvasive imaging with various contrasts. Advanced techniques like CEST measure metabolic information such as pH, while ^23^Na MRI assesses sodium tissue concentration, sensitive to pathological changes. Multinuclear interleaved techniques reduce scan time by combining ^1^H and ^23^Na imaging sequences. The interlaced pulse sequence, developed for clinical scanners, simultaneously maps acidity and sodium signals, reducing scan time by 46% without bias. In dynamic studies, this approach revealed distinct muscle responses during exercise and recovery, showing potential for functional studies without hardware modifications.
