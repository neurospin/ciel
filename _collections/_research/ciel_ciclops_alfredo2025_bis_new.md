title: New paper on super-resolution sodium MRI of human gliomas at 3T using physicsbased generative artificial intelligence
cat: ciel
subcat: ciclops
teasing: Alfredo L. Lopez Kolkovsky investigated how physics based generative artificial intelligence can boost the spatial resolution of sodium imaging at 3T applied to image brain tumors. This novel high-resolution synthetic-sodium MRI at 3T, enables clinically attainable multinuclear imaging for brain tumors that retain the inherent information from the native sodium. The resulting synthetic sodium significantly correlates with tissue expression, potentially supporting its utility as a non-invasive marker of underlying sodium homeostasis in brain tumors.
